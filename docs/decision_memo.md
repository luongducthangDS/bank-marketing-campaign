# Decision Memo: Eliminating Call Fatigue and Implementing the "3-Strike Policy"

**To:** Head of Telemarketing & Chief Marketing Officer  
**From:** Data Analyst  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Subject:** Conversion diminishing returns, macroeconomic sensitivity, and call center labor optimization  

---

## Executive Summary
Across **41,188 outbound sales contacts**, the telemarketing campaign achieved an **11.27% conversion rate** (4,640 term deposit subscribers), generating an estimated €426.9k in simulated net profit.

However, analysis of contact sequence reveals severe **diminishing returns (call fatigue)**:
- **88.0% of all successful subscriptions occur within the first 3 calls.**
- Beyond call 3, conversion rate plummets from **13.0% on call 1 down to 5.8% on call 4, and <2.5% past call 6**.
- Calling the same lead 4 to 56 times consumes **18.5% of total call volume (7,636 dials)** and **€24,800 in call center labor**, yet yields only 12% of total conversions with a CAC that is **3.2x higher** than the initial contacts.

---

## Key Quantitative Evidence

| Finding | Metric |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Contacts & Conversions** | **41,188 dials** $\to$ **4,640 conversions (11.27%)** |
| **Simulated Financials** | Labor Cost: **€129,869** \| Gross Value: **€556,800** \| Net Profit: **€426,931** (ROI = 328%) |
| **Diminishing Returns Curve** | Call 1: **13.0%** \| Call 2: **11.5%** \| Call 3: **10.8%** \| Call 4: **5.8%** \| Call 7+: **2.2%** |
| **The 3-Strike Threshold** | Calls $\le$ 3 capture **88.0% of total conversions** using 81.5% of effort |
| **Macroeconomic Elasticity** | When Euribor 3M is <2.0%, conversion rate is **23.4%**; when Euribor rises >4.0%, conversion drops to **4.4%** (-81% collapse) |
| **Channel Efficiency** | Cellular contacts convert at **14.7%** vs. Landline Telephone at **5.2%** (2.8x higher efficacy) |
| **Previous Campaign Lift** | Prior campaign success boosts subscription odds by **1.85x** ($p < 0.001$) |

---

## Action Plan & Financial Recommendations

1. **Enforce Hard-Cap "3-Strike Policy" (Immediate):**
   - Automatically close and recycle any lead that fails to convert after 3 unanswered/unsuccessful attempts within a single campaign window.
   - Prevents rep burnout, eliminates customer harassment, and frees 7,600+ dialing slots for fresh leads.
2. **Prioritize Cellular & Mobile Outreach (Month 1):**
   - Shift dialer configuration to prioritize mobile numbers first. Restrict landline calls to older demographic tranches where landline response is positive.
3. **Macro-Aware Campaign Timing (Ongoing):**
   - Synchronize aggressive term deposit telemarketing pushes during quarters when market interest rate expectations are stabilizing or falling (<3.0%), avoiding periods of peak interbank lending rates when retail bank deposits face fierce competition.

**Financial Impact:**  
Reallocating the 7,636 wasted "excess call" slots to fresh, warm leads is projected to generate **650 to 900 incremental conversions**, delivering **€78,000 to €108,000 in additional net profit** with zero added labor budget.
